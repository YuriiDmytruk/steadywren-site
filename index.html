How I work

  • Clean, typed data

    Numbers are numbers, dates are ISO, missing values are empty - ready for a spreadsheet or a database.

  • No silent failures

    If a site changes or blocks us, the run fails loudly instead of returning an empty success. You never pay for garbage.

  • Checked every day

    Every product runs a daily health check, so breakages are caught before you notice them.

  • Fair pricing

    Pay per result, with a results limit on every product to cap your cost.

  • Public data only

    No logins and no personal data of private individuals.

  • Maintained, not abandoned

    When a site changes, products are updated - and every question gets a real answer from the person who built it.
